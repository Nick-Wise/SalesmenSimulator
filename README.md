### Salesmen Simulator

Learning C# Fundamentals through making a game

### Base Idea

You are car sales men you only have 3 cars to start with and your all by yourself the more cars you sell, the more cars you can buy, the nicer store you can have, and the more employees you can hire.

You get all types of customers, each with different budgets and needs for their car. Some customers are more difficult to sell than others.

- Review system - The hire your store rating the less difficult customers become
- Better store - increases customer budget, decreases the amount cars cost the store
